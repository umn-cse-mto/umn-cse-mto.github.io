01 / OUR NEXT CHAPTER

Built on a legacy.
Focused on what’s next.

MTO is being revitalized under co-directors Seongjin Choi and Raphael Stern, bringing together complementary expertise in transportation AI, traffic modeling, and control.

Following several years of paused activity, we are opening a new chapter for the observatory: one that builds on its longstanding role in evaluating transportation management strategies and traveler information technologies.

Our ambition is to connect what we observe on the road with models, tools, and decisions that make transportation work better for everyone.

TRY IT · ILLUSTRATIVE SIMULATION

One automated car
can calm a traffic jam.

Twenty-two cars circle a ring road. Human drivers react to the car ahead with small delays and noise, and stop-and-go waves appear with no bottleneck at all. Add an automated vehicle (gold) that drives at the average speed instead of chasing gaps, and watch the waves fade.
