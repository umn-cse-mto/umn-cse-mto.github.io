What’s new.

Talks, papers, people, and milestones from the observatory.

  • Sep 2026

    MTO website relaunched

    The Minnesota Traffic Observatory opens its next chapter under co-directors Seongjin Choi and Raphael Stern.

  • Aug 2026

    Invited talk at the Korea Transport Institute

    Seongjin Choi presented “Can an LLM Understand Traffic Network?” at KOTI in Sejong, South Korea.

  • Jul 2026

    Guoliang Feng joins as a postdoctoral researcher

    Feng works on AI integration in traffic operations, agentic traffic simulation, and synthetic population generation.

  • Jun 2026

    Three new papers

    PMA-Diffusion is published in Transportation Research Part C; Weaver (TR Part C) and TrajFlow (Artificial Intelligence for Transportation) are in press.

  • Jun 2026

    Invited talk at the BIRS workshop in Banff

    Choi presented physics-guided diffusion priors for traffic state estimation at “Emerging Challenges in Statistical Modeling for Transportation Research.”

  • Apr 2026

    Undergraduate research: TMC-Agent and HighwayVLM

    Through UROP, Leo Curtis advanced TMC-Agent, an agentic AI system for traffic management, and Ismail Yusuf built HighwayVLM for highway incident detection.

  • Jan 2026

    Eight papers at TRB 2026

    The Choi group presented eight papers at the 105th Transportation Research Board Annual Meeting in Washington, D.C.

  • Dec 2025

    Preparing for AI use in transportation

    Choi joined a CTS webinar with MnDOT on how AI supports transportation work today. CTS article ↗

  • Fall 2025

    Hans Fischer Fellowship for Raphael Stern

    Stern was selected for a Hans Fischer Fellowship at the Technical University of Munich to study transportation network resilience and recovery from disruptive events. CEGE news ↗

  • Nov 2025

    Invited talk at UC Berkeley PATH

    Choi gave an invited talk in the PATH seminar series.

  • Oct 2025

    Transportation research in the era of generative AI

    Choi spoke at the NCITE/CTS Symposium in Minneapolis.
