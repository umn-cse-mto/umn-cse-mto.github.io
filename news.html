MTOSep 2026

The Minnesota Traffic Observatory opens its next chapter

Under co-directors Seongjin Choi and Raphael Stern, MTO is connecting what we observe on Minnesota roads with AI, traffic science, and control. The new site brings together our funded projects, publications, and people in one place.
