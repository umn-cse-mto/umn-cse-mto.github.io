CO-DIRECTOR · MINNESOTA TRAFFIC OBSERVATORY

Seongjin Choi

Assistant Professor in Civil, Environmental, and Geo-Engineering at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and transportation: generative models, vision-language-action models, and LLM agents.

Biography

Choi's research spans urban mobility data analytics, spatiotemporal data modeling, deep learning and AI, and connected automated vehicles and cooperative ITS. His work draws insights from urban mobility data and applies AI, including diffusion and flow-based generative models, vision-language-action models, and large language model agents, to real transportation problems, with the goal of more sustainable and efficient transportation systems.

Research

  • Generative intelligence for transportation modeling. Diffusion, normalizing flows, GANs, and probabilistic mixtures for traffic state estimation from sparse observations, trajectory generation, and probabilistic forecasting.
  • AI-powered connected and automated driving. Vision-language-action models, deep reinforcement learning for cooperative driving and vehicle control, and vision-language approaches for traffic surveillance.
  • LLM-powered traffic management. AI agents that support Traffic Management Center operators by interpreting network data and enabling natural-language interaction with traffic management systems.
