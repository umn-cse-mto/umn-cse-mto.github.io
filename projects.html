TMC-Agent

An agentic AI system that digests traffic detector data, aggregated probe-vehicle speeds, and camera video streams to give traffic management center operators actionable recommendations and real-time status reports.

CHOI GROUP · DATA QUALITY

Detector health monitoring on I-35W

Benchmarking fault-detection methods for loop-detector data on the I-35W corridor under controlled fault scenarios, to flag faulty sensors before their data reach operations.
